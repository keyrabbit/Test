The twelve spaces

Read from docs/decisions/ADR-007-twelve-spaces.md. Each space owes the game one kind of question that no other space asks; the twelfth asks for all of them at once.

#spacesizethe question it asksstate
1 Flat 14 full combinatorial, from a person's life playable
2 L'atrio full shape match, no text playable
3 Il cortile full correspondence — which cellar belongs to 14 playable
4 La cantina full light-dependent perception playable
5 L'ascensore small mechanical sequence playable
6 La portineria small cross-referencing records playable
7 La rimessa full continuity — what is connected to what playable
8 La scala full vertical — the stairs stop being scenery playable
9 Il sottotetto small sound to build
10 La terrazza full sightline to build
11 L'archivio small language to build
12 La strada full synthesis to build
